05Launch

Project Overview

A self-initiated mobile finance app concept focused on simplicity, usability and clean user flows.

Design Direction

The interface uses a dark product UI with bright cyan and blue accents to create strong hierarchy without making the experience feel heavy. Every major action is kept close to the user's thumb zone.
